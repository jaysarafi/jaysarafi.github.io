01About

I am a Visiting Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at Williams College. I received my PhD in Economics from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in May 2026.

I work on international macro finance. My research asks how U.S. monetary policy and capital flows reach firms in emerging markets, and how the maturity structure of their debt shapes that transmission.

Research fields: International Finance, International Macroeconomics, Monetary Policy, Sovereign Debt, Development Economics
